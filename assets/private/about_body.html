About RhymeCrime

RhymeCrime finds semantically related rhymes. For example, if you input pirate, RhymeCrime will give you rhymes like 
  anchored / tankard
  buggery / skulduggery / thuggery
  crypto / tiptoe
  marauder / otter / rotter / water
Every word in the list is thematically related to pirate in some way, and each word rhymes with the other words on the same line.

Thumbs-Up / Thumbs-Down

Figuring out which words are related is more of an art than a science. You can help RhymeCrime improve by pressing thumbs-up on words that are definitely related to the word you entered, and pressing thumbs-down on words that are definitely not related to the word you entered.

What's that second optional box for?

I'm glad you asked! The second box finds rhyming pairs where the first rhyme is related to the first word you entered, and the second rhyme is related to the second word you entered. For example, if you enter "food" in the first box and "evil" in the second box, RhymeCrime will give you rhymes like 
  cuisine / mean
  dinner / sinner
  rice / vice
  wheat / deceit
Each pair of words rhymes, each word on the left side is thematically related to food in some way, and each word on the right side is thematically related to evil.
